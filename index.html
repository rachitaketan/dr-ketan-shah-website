About Dr. Ketan Shah

Dr. Ketan Shah is a board-certified gastroenterologist dedicated to providing thoughtful, attentive care for patients seeking colonoscopy screening and surveillance. With years of experience in digestive health, Dr. Shah focuses on clear communication, patient comfort, and individualized guidance throughout the screening process.

Dr. Shah is highly experienced in colon cancer screening and prevention. He is especially interested in high-quality colonoscopy and maintains a high adenoma (polyp) detection rate of over 60%, compared with a national average of 20%. Research has shown that patients of colonoscopists with higher detection rates have a lower risk of developing colon cancer, advanced-stage colon cancer, or dying from colon cancer.

Dr. Shah also employs numerous innovative colonoscopy techniques, such as underwater colonoscopy, underwater polyp removal, endoscopic mucosal resection, and use of the Endocuff device to achieve higher patient comfort and success rates. He is also very experienced at performing unsedated colonoscopies, allowing select patients to comfortably observe their own exam, avoid the risks of sedation, and drive and return to work after their procedure.

All Colonoscopies Include:

  • High-resolution digital copies of your procedure and biopsy reports emailed to you
  • Polyp detection rate in the top 1% in the country, resulting in minimal chance of missed lesions and developing colorectal cancer
  • Specialty in removal of large polyps if found, as opposed to being referred to another provider

Frequently Asked Questions

Open-access colonoscopy is generally designed for healthy patients who need a routine screening or surveillance colonoscopy and don't have complex medical issues that require an in-person consultation beforehand. A questionnaire helps determine if this option is right for you.

Concierge colonoscopy offers a more personalized experience, including telemedicine consultations before and after your procedure, more direct communication with Dr. Shah, and priority scheduling when available.

Some patients may be candidates for an unsedated colonoscopy. This option can be discussed with Dr. Shah as part of the concierge service to determine whether it's appropriate for your situation.

Coverage varies by insurance plan and the reason for your colonoscopy (screening vs. diagnostic). We recommend checking with your insurance provider, and our team can also help answer questions about coverage.

Dr. Shah will review your responses to determine whether you meet the criteria for open-access scheduling. If you do, you'll receive a confirmation along with detailed pre-procedure instructions by email.

Preparation typically involves dietary changes and a bowel prep regimen in the days before your procedure. You'll receive specific, easy-to-follow instructions tailored to your scheduled appointment.

Answers above are general in nature. Final recommendations always depend on a medical review of your individual health history.
